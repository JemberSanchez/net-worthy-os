He pumped gas and cleaned floors his whole life. He died with eight million dollars. No big salary. No hot tips. No tech stocks. Just three boring habits, for sixty years. He bought companies that paid him to hold them, and reinvested every dividend into more shares. Sixty years later, eight million dollars. He put in a little at a time. Everything past that number is dividends buying more dividends. The machine, not the paycheck. Now the part nobody says. He held ninety-five stocks so one collapse couldn't sink him, and some did cut their dividends. And he never had to sell. Sixty years, no emergency big enough to touch it. That is the rare part, not the picking. You don't need his eight million. The engine is the same at any size. Own things that pay you, reinvest, and don't interrupt it. So, skill, or just time? Could you do it on a gas station paycheck? Team skill, or team time?
