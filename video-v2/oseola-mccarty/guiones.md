# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `vida`

> Miss McCarty, a washerwoman, handed a university one hundred fifty thousand dollars.
> You might guess she studied there. She left school in the sixth grade.
> For three-quarters of a century, she washed and ironed other people's clothes.
> She never owned a car. She walked everywhere.
> One TV channel. Most of what she earned, she saved.
> Then she gave more than half of it away.
> The campus was blocks from her house. She had never set foot on it.
> Her story is rare. That saving cost her decades of comfort.
> She still said she didn't regret one penny.
> Forty-four students have since earned scholarships at that university.

### `misterio`

> Oseola McCarty, a Mississippi woman who lived blocks from a university, never set foot on its campus. Then she funded it.
> You might expect a rich alumna. She left school in the sixth grade.
> She washed and ironed clothes for three-quarters of a century.
> No car. One TV channel. She walked everywhere and saved what she earned.
> Then she gave the university a hundred and fifty thousand dollars.
> Local businesses matched it.
> Her story is rare, and that saving cost her decades of comfort.
> She said she didn't regret one penny.
> Forty-four students later studied at that university on her savings.
