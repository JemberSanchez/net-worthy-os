# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `misterio`

> Six point three billion dollars, gone in one night.
> This week, hackers drained Binance, the world's biggest crypto exchange.
> It's the largest crypto theft on record.
> And they didn't rush.
> Investigators say they prepared for thirty-six hours and rehearsed first.
> Then, in four minutes, two hundred thirty-three million XRP left.
> Here's the part nobody tells you.
> They didn't crack a vault. They drained hot wallets, kept online for withdrawals.
> About three billion is frozen, and Binance says it will cover users.
> Until withdrawals reopen, your balance there is an IOU.
> So where would you keep six point three billion: an exchange, or your own wallet?
