# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `vida`

> Apple's third founder walked away for the price of your phone.
> Ronald Wayne wrote the contract, drew the logo, and owned a tenth.
> Then Jobs borrowed to fill their first order.
> In a partnership, each partner owes it all.
> Wayne was the only one with assets to lose.
> That same month, he quit.
> His price: eight hundred dollars.
> Years later, he sold those papers for five hundred.
> They fetched one point six million.
> That tenth would be hundreds of billions today, on paper.
> But he couldn't see the iPhone. He could see the debt.
> With your savings on the line, would you have walked away too?

## Puertas del ganador

- ✓ G-GANCHO: se entiende sin contexto
- ✓ G-TU: espectador en la palabra 10 (≤19)
- ✓ G-CIFRAS: 3 cifras dichas (≤3): eight hundred; five hundred; one point six million
- ✓ G-COMODIN: sin frases comodín
- ✓ G-CIERRE: cierre «pregunta»
- ✓ G-LARGO: 106 palabras (85-110) ≈ 34.2 s
- ✓ G-RELLENO: sin relleno
- ✓ G-GIRO: hueco máx. sin giro 7.4 s (≤10 s)
- ✓ G-BUCLE: enlaza por: away, walked
