# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `contradiccion`

> Curtis Priem, a co-founder of the chip giant inside your index fund, sold every single share.
> That giant is Nvidia. Today its boss, Jensen Huang, quit, and the stock slid.
> Priem was Nvidia's first chief technology officer.
> He retired early and sold out.
> Had he held, Forbes says, he'd be worth seventy billion dollars.
> His actual fortune? About thirty million.
> The worst sale in tech history, right?
> Not to him. He gave forty million to his old college.
> Hindsight cheats. Back then, Nvidia's rival looked unbeatable.
> It went bust, and Nvidia bought what was left.
> Nobody gets to sell only the losers.
> So, would you have held every share?
