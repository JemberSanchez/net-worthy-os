# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `contradiccion`

> Warren Buffett, the world's most famous investor, said he could end America's deficit in five minutes. You won't guess how.
> No tax hike. No spending cut. One law.
> Any year the deficit tops three percent of the economy,
> every sitting member of Congress is banned from re-election.
> Last year's deficit? Two trillion dollars.
> That's about double his line. Under his law, nobody in Congress keeps their seat.
> The honest part: he said it with a laugh, live on CNBC. His own company called it not a serious proposal.
> The point was incentives. People fix what costs them their job.
> Remember his five minutes next time you vote.

> Warren Buffett, the billionaire investor, had a five-minute fix for the deficit. Congress would hate it, and you might love it.
> July twenty eleven. A live TV interview in Idaho.
> He said: pass one law. If the deficit tops three percent of the economy,
> every sitting member of Congress can't run again.
> Fast forward. Last year, America borrowed two trillion dollars.
> Interest on the debt now costs more than the military.
> The catch: Buffett was joking. His company said it was never a serious plan.
> His real point? When nobody pays for failure, failure keeps happening.
> Remember those five minutes next time you vote.
