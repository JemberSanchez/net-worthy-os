# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `cuenta`

> You could call the 1929 crash and still go bankrupt. One man did.
> Jesse Livermore traded behind a locked door with no name.
> In early 1929, he bet against the whole boom.
> By spring, he was down six million dollars.
> Then October hit. Legend says he made a hundred million.
> The public blamed him, so he hired a bodyguard.
> Under five years later, he filed for bankruptcy, his mansion gone.
> And that hundred million? TIME's story on his bankruptcy never mentions it.
> Betting against a market can cost you more than you put in.
> He called the crash. That hundred million is still a legend.
