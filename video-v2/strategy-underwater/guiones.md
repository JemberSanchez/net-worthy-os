# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `contradiccion+g1`

> Strategy, a software company turned bitcoin vault, is now losing money on its bitcoin.
> If you own an index fund, you may own a piece of it.
> It paid about seventy-five thousand dollars per bitcoin.
> This week, bitcoin fell near sixty-four thousand.
> The company built to never sell is underwater.
> Now MSCI is dropping it from its indexes.
> Funds that copy those indexes must sell the stock.
> Strategy doesn't have to sell a single bitcoin.
> But raising cash to buy more just got harder.
> Nobody knows where bitcoin goes next. This is not advice.
> That vault holds about one of every twenty-five bitcoin that will ever exist.
