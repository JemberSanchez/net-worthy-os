# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `misterio`

> Anne Scheiber, an IRS auditor, never earned over four thousand a year.
> She died with twenty-two million. You've heard the legend.
> Five thousand dollars in savings, a cheap apartment, and decades of blue-chip stocks.
> It's a perfect story. It's also missing a piece.
> Her executor found a tax return from years before she retired.
> She was already collecting stock dividends back then.
> Her real starting pile was likely several times bigger.
> So her return? Roughly the market's average.
> That's the honest version, and it's better news for you.
> She didn't need genius. She needed decades.
> Start early, because time did for Anne Scheiber what her salary never could.
