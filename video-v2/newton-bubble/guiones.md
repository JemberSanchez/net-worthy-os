# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `contradiccion`

> You would never buy a stock at the top. Isaac Newton did.
> He ran the Royal Mint.
> In April seventeen twenty, he decided the South Sea boom would end badly, and sold.
> His profit was about twenty thousand pounds.
> But the stock kept climbing without him.
> By June, he put nearly everything back in, almost at the peak.
> By October, the stock was worth under a quarter of that peak.
> He lost the whole profit, and a good bit more.
> He was still rich. Only with a third less than before the bubble.
> Even Newton bought at the top. Seeing the bubble was the easy part.
