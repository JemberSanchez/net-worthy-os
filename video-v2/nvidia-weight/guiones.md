# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `error`

> Monday, the stock market soared. A giant inside your index fund sank.
> Nvidia fell four percent after its board opened an investigation into its own CEO.
> Your fund still had a great day.
> An index fund protects you from one company, right?
> Not quite. Nvidia alone is over six percent of the S and P five hundred.
> That's over six cents of every dollar on one company.
> That weight lifted your fund on the way up.
> It pulls the same way down, and one headline is enough.
> Nobody knows how the investigation ends, and one day proves nothing.
> An index fund spreads your money. It just doesn't spread it evenly.

- ✓ G-CIFRAS: 3 cifras dichas (≤3): four; six; five hundred
- ✓ G-COMODIN: sin frases comodín
- ✓ G-CIERRE: cierre «afirmacion»
- ✓ G-LARGO: 110 palabras (85-110) ≈ 35.5 s
- ✓ G-RELLENO: sin relleno
