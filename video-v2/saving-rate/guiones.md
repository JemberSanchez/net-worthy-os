# Guiones — crítico de guion (PROVISIONAL)

**Ganador:** `pregunta`

> Three dollars and eighty cents. That's what Americans saved from every hundred they took home in September.
> The official saving rate: three point eight percent.
> So where did the rest go?
> Spending jumped zero point five percent in one month.
> Income? Up just zero point two.
> Spending grew more than twice as fast as paychecks.
> Here's the part nobody tells you.
> That spending is part of why the economy looks strong right now.
> But a strong economy built on a thin cushion is fragile.
> So are you saving more than three dollars and eighty cents of every hundred, or less?

## Puertas del ganador

- ✓ G-CIFRA: cifra en la 1.ª frase
- ✓ G-LARGO: 100 palabras (85-110) ≈ 32.3 s
- ✓ G-RELLENO: sin relleno
- ✓ G-GIRO: hueco máx. sin giro 5.8 s (≤10 s)
- ✓ G-BUCLE: enlaza por: cent, dollar, every
