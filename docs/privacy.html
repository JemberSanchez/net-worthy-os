Privacy Policy — Net Worthy Publisher

Last updated: 2026-07-31

"Net Worthy Publisher" is a personal-use application built by the owner of the Net Worthy (@networthytv) Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is used exclusively by that owner to publish video content to their own Facebook Page and their own linked Instagram professional account, via the Meta Graph API.

Who can use this app

This app is not distributed to or used by the public. It only accesses Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts that belong to, or are administered by, the app's own owner and developer.

What data this app accesses

The app requests the minimum permissions needed to publish video content on behalf of the Page/Instagram account owner: basic Page/account identification, and the ability to upload and publish video posts (Reels) to that Page and Instagram account. The app does not read, collect, or store data about third-party users, followers, or visitors.

What data this app stores

The app stores, locally and privately, the access tokens needed to publish on behalf of the Page/Instagram account owner. No data is shared with, sold to, or made accessible to any third party. No advertising or tracking technology is used.

Data deletion

The Page/Instagram account owner can revoke this app's access at any time from their Meta Account Center ("Apps and websites" settings) or from the app's own settings, which immediately deletes any locally stored access tokens.
