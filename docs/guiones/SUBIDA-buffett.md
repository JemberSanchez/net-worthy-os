## DESCRIPCIÓN (pegar tal cual)
Warren Buffett bought his first stock at 11. He didn't have a billion dollars until 56.

That 45-year gap is the whole point. He didn't get rich late because he started late — he got rich
late because 40 years of compounding finally got big enough to see.

$100 invested with Berkshire in 1965 became about $5.5 million 60 years later. Berkshire returned
~20% a year; the market returned ~10.4% (before inflation) — not even double, but over 60 years
that's the difference between $39,000 and $5.5 million.

You will never get Buffett's 20%. You don't need it. His edge wasn't the return — it was the clock.
